Doctor Who TARDIS Sightings in London

Doctor Who is one of the longest-running and most iconic science fiction series in television history. It follows the adventures of the Doctor, a mysterious alien Time Lord from the planet Gallifrey who travels through time and space in a ship called the TARDIS—which looks like a British police box on the outside. Along the way, the Doctor often takes on human companions, battles monsters and alien threats, and stands up for justice and compassion across the universe.

I enjoy Doctor Who because it combines imagination, history, and futuristic storytelling in a unique and meaningful way. Each Doctor brings their own personality to the role, and I love how the show constantly evolves while still staying true to its core themes of hope, curiosity, and doing the right thing. The show's ability to tackle complex moral issues while still being fun and adventurous is what keeps me watching.
